A cubic diameter bound for the motion of primitive distance-regular graphs
Machine-assisted proof draft, July 23, 2026

Main statement
--------------
Let X be a primitive distance-regular graph on n vertices with diameter d >= 3.
Then X is a Johnson graph, a Hamming graph, or

    motion(X) >= n/(14 d^3).

Status
------
Complete unrefereed proof draft. Every new implication is written out in the PDF.
The proof imports explicitly cited published theorems of Pyber-Skresanov and Kivva;
Section 9 of the PDF contains a dependency/hypothesis ledger. The exact-arithmetic
script checks scalar and discrete inequalities but does not machine-verify the
imported graph-theoretic theorems.
